Open Positions

Applications are invited from highly self-driven, commercially minded and relationship-oriented candidates for positions within Index Group’s Corporate Relations & Strategic Partnerships function.

Field Connector

The face of Index Group in the market. You identify, engage and build meaningful relationships with business owners, executives and decision-makers across assigned territories. You own your territory and are rewarded for the value those relationships create.

Relationship Building Territory Ownership Performance-Based

Corporate Relations Associate

Support the development and management of high-value corporate relationships. You will help manage stakeholder communication, prepare briefing materials, coordinate engagement activities, and ensure consistent, professional interaction with key partners.

Stakeholder Management Communication Relationship Nurturing

Business Development & Partnerships Associate

Identify and pursue partnership and sponsorship opportunities. You will support the corporate pipeline, assist with proposals and presentations, and help convert opportunities into long-term strategic collaborations.

Partnership Development Commercial Awareness Pipeline Management

Events & Corporate Engagement Associate

Support the planning and execution of Index Group’s corporate events, profiling sessions and engagement activities. You will help create high-quality experiences that strengthen relationships with partners, nominees and stakeholders.

Event Coordination Experience Design Partner Engagement
