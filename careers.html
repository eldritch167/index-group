You connect. We create impact.

Field Connectors are the face of Index Group in their communities. You identify, engage and build relationships with business owners, executives, professionals and entrepreneurs who are shaping Botswana’s future.

Own your territory.

As a Field Connector, you will be assigned a defined territory or relationship environment (e.g. malls, business districts, events, professional networks, institutions).

You own that territory. You build the relationships within it. And you are rewarded for the value those relationships create.
